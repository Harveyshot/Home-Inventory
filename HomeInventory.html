Use It On Your Phone

Works great on iPhone & Android

Home Inventory runs right in your phone's browser — no App Store needed. Add it to your home screen and it works just like a real app, with full camera access for photos and receipts.

1

Transfer the file

After downloading, save HomeInventory.html to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or email it to yourself.

2

Open in Safari

On your iPhone, open the file from iCloud Drive or your email. Make sure to use Safari for best results.

3

Add to Home Screen

Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari, then tap Add to Home Screen. It'll appear as an app icon.

4

Start using it

Tap the icon to open. Use Take Photo to snap items directly with your camera — no file picker needed.

Android users: Open the file in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu, and choose Add to Home Screen. Works the same way.
